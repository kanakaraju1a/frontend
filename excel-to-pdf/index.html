Excel to PDF converter

Convert Excel workbooks and spreadsheet files to PDF online. FileFlow supports XLS, XLSX, XLSM, ODS and CSV files for reports, tables, charts and printable worksheets.

Preserve spreadsheet appearance

The converter uses LibreOffice Calc or Microsoft Excel to render worksheet content, displayed formula results, colors, borders, charts, print areas and existing page settings into PDF.

Batch Excel conversion

Upload one or more spreadsheets and download a separate PDF for every workbook. Files are processed temporarily by the configured converter service and removed after each request.

How to convert Excel to PDF

  1. Upload XLS, XLSX, XLSM, ODS or CSV files.
  2. Start the Excel to PDF conversion.
  3. Download each generated PDF and review page breaks.
