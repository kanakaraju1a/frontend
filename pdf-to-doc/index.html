PDF to DOC converter

Convert PDF files into layout-preserving DOCX Word documents. Exact-layout mode keeps fonts, positions, images, colors and visible form controls in place.

Exact visual output

Each PDF page is rendered as a high-quality image inside DOCX. The page appearance is preserved, but individual text and controls are not directly editable.

Original layout

Complex columns, radio buttons, images and tables remain visible in their PDF positions. Always review the DOCX after conversion.

Questions

Can I edit the text?

Not in exact-layout mode. Use OCR to extract text from scanned or image-based pages.

Does it support images?

Yes. All visible page content is preserved in the rendered DOCX page.
